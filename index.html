Why invest

Why we think students will buy it

Most students bring food from home because buying lunch every day is expensive. At lunchtime there is usually one microwave, a long queue, and half the break is gone. Many end up eating their food cold.

A home-cooked meal, warm, wherever you're sitting.

PocketHeat makes the cheap option the pleasant one. Bringing leftovers becomes the easy choice, which means cheaper lunches for students and less food thrown away.

Every euro raised is operating budget. It goes straight into making, shipping and launching PocketHeat.

    Questions

    Questions families ask us

    Still unsure? Ask the student who shared this page, or email . We are happy to explain.

    What exactly do I get for €20?

    You become a shareholder of PocketHeat and receive regular updates on how the company is doing.

    Is the lunchbox really cordless?

    Yes. A built-in 7200 mAh battery powers the heating, so it does not need a socket while it heats. You charge it beforehand with the charging cable in the box.

    What are the four heat settings for?

    Different meals need different amounts of heat. You pick one of four settings with the button on the lid, and the display shows which one is on.

    Is it safe for food?

    The container that touches your food is 304 stainless steel, a common food-safe steel. The outer case is food-grade PP plastic.

    What is my money used for?

    All of it is operating budget: manufacturing the lunchboxes, shipping them to the Netherlands, packaging and branding, and launch marketing.

    Can I lose my money?

    Yes. If sales are lower than we hope, your share can lose some or all of its value. Share purchases are final and cannot be refunded, so only invest an amount you are comfortable with.

    Is this a regulated investment?

    No. PocketHeat is a student company run as part of a course. The shares are part of that project and are not a regulated investment product.

    Why does the payment go to Julia?

    Julia Agnieszka Kusmierska is our CEO. Our Revolut payment page and bank account are in her name, so that is the name you see when you pay.

    Do I need a Revolut account?

    No. Our Revolut payment page also accepts a normal bank card, and you can always pay by bank transfer from any Dutch or European bank.

    Why at most 2 shares per person?

    We want the company to be owned by many supporters, so no single shareholder can own a large part of it.

    What do you do with my details?

    We keep your name, email address and payment details in our shareholder register. We use them only for this project.
